\resumeSection{Operating Model}
\begin{resumeItems}
  \item Assess the technology, staffing needs, business constraints, and daily operations together. Fix the gaps that create the most risk or slow delivery first.
  \item Set a long-term technical plan, define ownership, and identify the roles or skills needed to carry it.
  \item Put missing operating foundations in place, including documentation, security procedures, access management, incident response, disaster recovery, and service ownership.
  \item Use product, system, and team performance data to surface problems early and give leadership a clearer basis for difficult decisions.
